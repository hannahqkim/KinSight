What happens
An adult child installs KinSight, links the elder's phone, picks a family safe word and adds trusted contacts.
A number not in contacts rings. KinSight prompts: "Open KinSight before you answer."
The caller tells a story. Speech becomes text live and the call is scored for fraud as it goes.
KinSight shows one question to ask out loud, like the safe word or a detail only family would know.
The whole screen turns peach or teal with one instruction. Family hears about likely scams.
Elder sees
A short welcome from their family, ideally in a familiar voice.
One big button: "Answer with KinSight." The call goes on speaker so the app can hear.
A live risk meter, the caller's words in large type, and what KinSight caught.
A yellow screen with the exact words to say, plus "They knew it" / "They didn't".
"Hang up" with one button, or "Looks safe" with the option to keep listening.
They feel
Family: relief that a plan exists. Elder: respected, not policed.
Curious, a little wary. The prompt should feel like a habit, not an alarm.
Pressure from the caller. The screen has to be the calmest thing in the room.
Back in control: they're testing the caller, not the other way round.
Safe and proud. Never embarrassed for having been targeted.
Design risk
Setup that feels like surveillance. Let the elder co-own the safe word.
Seconds to act, and a web app can't see incoming calls.
A jumpy meter adds stress. Smooth it and lead with words.
The caller hears the question. Phrase it as natural chat.
A false "safe" is the worst outcome. Make it softer than "Hang up".
