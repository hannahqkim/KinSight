How it works on iPhone
1
Natalie invites Margaret
From her own phone. Margaret taps yes.
2
Strangers reach KinSight first
iPhone silences unknown numbers. The carrier forwards them to KinSight's line instead of voicemail.
3
KinSight rings Margaret and listens
The call comes through the KinSight app, like a WhatsApp call, so KinSight can hear it.
4
Past 50%, Natalie is told
A push alert on Natalie's phone. Margaret sees one instruction.
iPhone lets a third-party app
Ring like a phone call and carry the audio (CallKit, VoIP push)
Get unknown callers through Silence Unknown Callers plus carrier call forwarding
Label or block numbers on the ring screen (Call Directory, Live Caller ID Lookup)
Send push alerts to Natalie
iPhone does not let any app
Listen to a normal phone call, even on speaker
Use the microphone while a normal call is on
Pop up a message when a normal call rings
Read the call log or the caller's number
So KinSight becomes the phone line for unknown numbers only. Contacts ring as normal and stay private. This needs a native iPhone app and a phone number service, not the web app alone.
